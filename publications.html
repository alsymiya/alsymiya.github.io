Selected Publications

Peer-reviewed journal papers and conference work. The first three have been deployed in MotionGen.

2024
First Author

A Unified Real-Time Motion Generation Algorithm for Approximate Position Analysis of Planar N-Bar Mechanisms

Zhijie Lyu, Wei Liao, Anurag Purwar

ASME Journal of Mechanical Design, June 2024, 146(6): 063302 (12 pages)

  • Extended the prismatic-joint approximation trick from textbook special cases to arbitrary combinations of joints.
  • Defined a standardized matrix-set data structure for kinematically simulated samples and analyzed its structural complexity.
  • Documents the core of the 2022–2025 MotionGen simulator.
  • Distilled from the author's M.S. thesis, Real-Time Kinematic Simulation of Planar Linkage Mechanisms (Stony Brook, August 2022) — the thesis contains the longer, original treatment.
PDF DOI M.S. Thesis
2024
First Author Featured Article

Deep Learning Conceptual Design of Sit-to-Stand Parallel Motion Six-Bar Mechanisms

Zhijie Lyu, Anurag Purwar

ASME Journal of Mechanical Design, July 2024, 147(1): 1–18

  • Improved an atlas-based, data-driven approach for planar linkage path synthesis through targeted data preprocessing.
  • Used a Variational Auto-Encoder (VAE) with three ranking metrics to rank candidate solutions retrieved from the dataset.
  • Selected as a featured article by editor Faez Ahmed (MIT).

2025
First Author

Point-based Models: A Unified Approach for Geometric Constraint Analysis of Planar N-Bar Mechanisms with Rotary, Sliding, and Rolling Joints

Zhijie Lyu, Anurag Purwar

ASME Journal of Mechanisms and Robotics, Special Issue for IDETC-CIE 2024

  • Developed methodology and data structures that extend the constraint-graph approach from two constraint types (distance, angle) to three (angle-angle), enabling implementation of wheels and gears in linkage systems.
  • Theoretical foundation: wheels can be represented as linkages, allowing all elements to be analyzed like a truss structure.

First Author

Construction, Reduction, and Decomposition of Planar Mechanisms via an Extended Pebble Game Framework

Zhijie Lyu, Anurag Purwar

ASME Journal of Mechanisms and Robotics, December 2025, 17(12): 121009 (15 pages)

  • Modified pebble-game framework that extends constraint-graph analysis from two constraint types (distance, angle) to three (angle-angle).
  • Analyzes planar geared linkage systems in polynomial time.
  • A polynomial-time algorithm for general mobility analysis, expanded from linkages to gears + linkages. Python prototype completed.

Path Generative Model Based on Conditional β-Variational Auto-Encoder for Four-Bar Mechanism Design

Anar Nurizada, Zhijie Lyu, Anurag Purwar

ASME Journal of Mechanisms and Robotics, June 2025, 17(6): 061004

2026
First Author Conference Paper

Data-Driven Kinematic Synthesis Using Differentiable Physics: Sampling Density for Reliable Initialization Into the Basin of Attraction

Zhijie Lyu, Suhas Gangireddy, Anurag Purwar

Proceedings of ASME IDETC/CIE 2026, August 23–26, 2026, Houston, Texas. Paper IDETC-195131.

  • Introduces a retrieve-decode-refine pipeline combining nearest-neighbor retrieval over 550,000 samples, a LLaMA-based sequence decoder, and a differentiable kinematic simulator.
  • Characterizes convergence basins across all 17 supported four-bar, Stephenson, and Watt mechanism topologies.
  • Achieves 3.34× faster refinement and a 5× precision-floor advantage with analytical-gradient L-BFGS over finite-difference Levenberg–Marquardt.
